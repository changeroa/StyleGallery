Process

Three days from wet clay to table.

Each piece passes the same three benches. Nothing leaves the studio without its maker's mark on the foot.

01 · Monday

Thrown on the wheel

Every form starts as a centered lump and a steady hand. Walls are pulled thin, then left to firm overnight.

02 · Tuesday

Trimmed and glazed

Feet are carved leather-hard, bisque-fired, then dipped by hand. Drips are kept; they tell you where the glaze pooled.

03 · Thursday

Fired overnight

The kiln climbs for eleven hours, peaks, and cools until Saturday morning, when the door opens on the new firing.

Peak temperature, cone 101,240°C
