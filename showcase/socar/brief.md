# SOCAR (쏘카), unofficial study

## Direction

Reference: the captured socar.kr home. Mood: clear, bright, utilitarian - white cards with hairline borders and one confident blue. Signature: the search box does something immediately; typing a place narrows the city grid below and announces how many matches remain.

## System

- Type: Pretendard Variable, 800 section titles at 20px with a grey one-line subtitle.
- Palette: ground `#ffffff`, blue `#0066e0`, sky `#e8f3ff`, ink `#1c1f23`, grey `#5c6470`, lines `#e6e9ee`.
- Motion density: low - card lift, button ready-state, accordion caret.

## Techniques

- Shared product-art kit for perk icons and painted trip scenes.
- Native `details` accordion for the FAQ.

## Layout Floor

Fixed translucent header; four-column grids go to two below 900px and the finder stacks below 760px.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 3 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/socar.jpg`).

- Matches: white fixed header with blue wordmark, three links, a pale-blue For Foreigners chip and login; a bold one-line promise over a date field, a wide place search, and a grey search button; two outlined "how to use" rows with chevrons; three perk cards with the first in pale blue; four image cards; a four-column city grid with blue "hot" tags; a row of trust badges; FAQ rows.
- Deliberate differences: typing in the place search filters the city/airport/station grid live and the button turns blue; photography is drawn scenes; all fares and places are examples.
