# Notion, unofficial study

## Direction

Reference: the captured notion.so home (Korean locale, 2026-09-21, 1440x900, 4,383px). Mood: quiet white paper, warm greys, one blue. Signature: the pill-highlighted word in the headline cycles through the jobs a page does, and an agent drops a new card onto the board as it scrolls into view.

## System

- Type: Noto Sans KR 800 headline at -0.045em; Noto Serif KR for quotes.
- Palette: ground `#ffffff`, warm `#f7f6f3`, ink `#191919`, secondary `#5a5a58`, blue `#0f6fd9`, soft blue `#e8f1fc`; duotone cards red `#c9463d`, blue `#2d5c9e`, amber `#8a5a0e`.
- Motion density: rotating verb, board card landing, bar growth, recording wave (visible only), logo marquee.

## Techniques

- Shared kit reveals, counters, and marquee.
- An original one-stroke doodle character; the brand's illustrations are not used.

## Layout Floor

Sticky header that gains a hairline on scroll; the mockup sidebar hides and the board goes to two columns below 820px; AI cards and quotes stack below 900px.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 5 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/notion.jpg`).

- Matches: white header with blue CTA; a centered oversized headline with a blue pill around one word; a one-line sub and blue plus soft-blue CTAs; a shadowed workspace mockup with a warm sidebar and a board; a hand-drawn character peeking over the mockup; a customer logo row; warm-grey AI cards with round black arrow buttons and a wide agent card; five use-case chips; three duotone testimonial cards with serif quotes; a stat ticker; a warm closing band with two CTAs; a footer with a serif quote and link columns.
- Deliberate differences: all copy, teams, and quotes are invented; the character is an original doodle; testimonial photos are abstract duotone light.
- Remaining gaps: study 3,351px vs 4,383px; the original's mockup carries far denser real UI.
