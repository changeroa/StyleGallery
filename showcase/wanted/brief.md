# Wanted, unofficial study

## Direction

Reference: the captured wanted.co.kr home. Mood: calm, confident, white with a single saturated blue and colorful 3D-ish icons. Signature: the company carousel pages by five with arrows that disable at the ends, and every company is an invented name set in type on its own color.

## System

- Type: Pretendard Variable, 800 section titles at 20px.
- Palette: ground `#ffffff`, blue `#0f5ae0`, ink `#171719`, grey `#5a5c63`, lines `#e9e9ec`, one color per invented company.
- Motion density: low - icon hover tilt, carousel slide, bookmark toggle.

## Techniques

- Shared product-art kit for icons and theme imagery; no real company marks.

## Layout Floor

Sticky frosted header and a fixed round chat button; `scroll-padding-bottom` keeps focus clear of it. Carousel pages by two below 900px.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 2 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/wanted.jpg`).

- Matches: frosted sticky header with the name, a menu, and outlined sign-up and business buttons; a centered notice pill; a ten-icon colorful quick menu; a "companies you want to work at" carousel of five wide colored cards with small arrows; a three-card news row with bottom captions; a black round chat button at bottom right.
- Deliberate differences: company cards carry invented names set in type on their own color instead of real logos and building photos; news imagery is drawn; the carousel really pages with disabled end states.
- Remaining gaps: the original's photographic news cards; study 1,903px vs 2,997px.
