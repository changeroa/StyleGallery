## Direction

Wise's homepage register: white ground, enormous heavy uppercase display type with very tight leading, sentence-case section titles in a neutral sans, lime as the only accent (primary pills and links on dark), forest green for text on light and for rounded display panels, and pale grey-green full-width bands.

## System

- Type: Inter 900 uppercase at 0.86 leading for display; Inter 600 for section titles; grey body.
- Color: ink #0e0f0c, lime #9fe870, forest #163300, band #e8ebe6; painted gradients for the app card and globe.
- Shape: full pills for buttons and chips, 28-36px radii on cards and panels, round currency dots; flags are replaced by plain colour dots and currency codes.
- Rates, fees, and figures are invented; the wordmark is plain lowercase text, not the logo.

## Techniques

- Fee-transparent converter: amount and currency changes recompute fee, converted amount, invented rate, and what arrives, in that reading order.
- Globe with currency coins that rise at different speeds with scroll, with a pause button like the capture's media control.
- A currency chip strip that drifts with scroll instead of looping.
- Forest panels with lime display type; alternating media and text rows; a painted-gradient app card with a decorative code pattern.

## Layout Floor

The header is not sticky, as in the capture. Every account, send, and country action is an honest demo button. Scroll-linked motion stops when paused and under reduced motion; nothing loops.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 8 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/wise.jpg`).

- Matches: white ground with centred heavy uppercase display type and a lime pill plus underlined link; a globe with currency coins under the hero with a pause control; a pale grey-green band with a single white feature card; a forest-green rounded panel with lime display type ("built for business" register); a three-part security section; alternating media and text rows; a second forest panel for the mission; a painted-gradient app card; a country link grid on the band; a light footer.
- Deliberate differences: the rendered globe and gold coins are CSS gradients; flags are colour dots; a working fee-transparent converter is added under the hero, which the capture does not show at that point; all copy and figures are original or invented.
- Remaining gaps: the hero headline wraps to three lines instead of two; the capture's photo media rows become drawn phone cards; study 6,829px vs 13,810px.
