# Loom, unofficial study

## Direction

Reference: the captured loom.com home (2026-09-21, 1440x900, 12,622px). Mood: cheerful, rounded, white with royal-blue slabs. Signature: the recorder in the hero actually records - its timer counts while you watch - and the bug ticket fills itself with console errors.

## System

- Type: Nunito Sans 900 at -0.035em for headlines, 400 body.
- Palette: white ground, blue `#1a5fd6`, soft blue `#e8f0ff`, ink `#14171f`, grey `#4f5563`, dark panel `#0d0f14`; pastel art lime, lilac, sky, butter.
- Motion density: split hero, recording dot and timer (paused offscreen), error drop-in, card lift, tab swap.

## Techniques

- Very large panel radii (28-36px) and all-pill buttons in three weights (blue, soft blue, black on blue).
- Alternating text/art feature rows.

## Layout Floor

Sticky promo plus nav with 130px scroll padding; bug panel and rows stack below 860px; use-case cards go 4, 2, 1 columns.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 8 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/loom.jpg`).

- Matches: a royal-blue promo strip over a white sticky nav with blue and soft-blue pills; a bold centered two-line headline with a grey sub and two pill CTAs over a large rounded video frame with a round webcam bubble; a centered logo headline with a CTA over a wordmark grid; a big blue rounded panel pairing a bug-report ticket with a white headline and a black pill; alternating feature rows (red record dot, calendar art, editor art with a struck-through caption, share art); a dark rounded panel; pastel use-case cards; a blue blog panel with two article cards and a black pill; a quote card and a closing CTA card.
- Deliberate differences: the video frame is a painted office with a slide and a live timer; the bug ticket fills with errors on arrival; use-case tabs swap cards; people are drawn, not photographed; copy, quotes, and customers are fictional; the cookie banner over every capture frame is omitted.
- Remaining gaps: study 6,226px vs 12,622px; the original's dark product hero with a desktop mockup is omitted.
