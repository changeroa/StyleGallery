# CLASS101, unofficial study

## Direction

Reference: the captured class101.net subscription landing. Mood: warm, energetic, orange on white with one bold red panel. Signature: the page never stops selling but stays readable - tiles drift in opposite directions, the chart line draws itself, and the same CTA appears in hero, closing, and a floating bar.

## System

- Type: Pretendard Variable, 800 centered titles, orange (`#c94700` text) emphasis on one phrase.
- Palette: ground `#ffffff`, header warmth `#fff6ef`, orange `#ff5f00 -> #e85200` CTA gradient, red panel `#e8112d`, review band `#f3f3f3`.
- Motion density: two opposed marquees (paused offscreen), chart draw on arrival and on tab change, CTA magnet.

## Techniques

- Shared kit marquee with a reversed second row.
- `scroll-padding-bottom` keeps focused elements clear of the fixed CTA bar.

## Layout Floor

Sticky two-row header and a fixed floating CTA bar; content and the study bar reserve space at the bottom. Perk tabs go to two columns below 760px.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 6 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/class101.jpg`).

- Matches: warm-tinted two-row header with category chips; centered small CLASS101+ label, two-line headline with an orange emphasized phrase, orange gradient CTA and a text link; two rows of category tiles; a red-title perk section with four tabs over a large red panel holding a white line chart with a red "3x" marker; a dense class card grid with overlaid titles and TOP badges; a review masonry; an FAQ; and a floating white CTA bar pinned to the bottom.
- Deliberate differences: tile rows marquee in opposite directions and the chart line draws itself; tile and class imagery is drawn, not photographed; every class and review is invented.
- Remaining gaps: the original's photo-rich tiles (faces, food) feel more human; study 3,536px vs 6,710px.
