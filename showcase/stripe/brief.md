# Stripe, unofficial study

## Direction

Reference: the captured stripe.com home. Mood: bright, engineered, generous; one indigo accent and one explosion of warm-to-violet color. Signature: the ribbon, the ticker, the starburst, the car on the aerial road, and the diagram packets all move, so the page feels like money in motion.

## System

- Type: Inter 300-500; two-tone headlines (ink first clause, slate `#50617a` continuation).
- Palette: ink/navy `#0a2540`, indigo `#533afd`, soft `#f6f9fc`, lines `#e3e8ee`; ribbon hues `#ffb86b -> #ff5f9e -> #a960ff -> #533afd -> #3ab8ff`.
- Motion density: ribbon, ticker, burst, usage bars, aerial car, architecture packets; all loops visibility-gated.

## Techniques

- Canvas ribbon and starburst; SVG architecture with animated packets.
- Hairline frame borders at the content column edges, as in the capture.

## Layout Floor

Header sits on the hero and scrolls away; the bento goes single-column below 900px; the cookie panel that covers every capture frame is omitted.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 8 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/stripe.jpg`).

- Matches: header on white with indigo CTAs, a live volume line above a large two-tone headline (ink then slate), indigo and outlined CTAs, a warm-to-violet ribbon sweeping the top right, a customer wordmark row, a soft blue-white section with a two-tone title and shadowed bento cards (checkout terminal plus order summary, billing card with usage bars), a centered stats row with vertical hairlines over a fan of thin lines, an enterprise block with an aerial road image, a navy developer section with an architecture diagram, and a news carousel.
- Deliberate differences: all copy, customers, stories, and numbers are new or fictional; the ribbon, fan, aerial car, and diagram packets animate; imagery is drawn.
- Remaining gaps: study 4,814px vs 14,668px; the original has more product sections and a large sitemap footer.
