# Catch Table (캐치테이블), unofficial study

## Direction

Reference: the captured app.catchtable.co.kr home, which renders a 480px mobile shell centered on a dark page. Signature: the study keeps that honest app-in-a-browser frame and makes it feel native - swipeable hero cards, horizontally scrolling rows, working price tabs, and save hearts.

## System

- Type: Pretendard Variable, 700 section titles at 18px.
- Palette: desk `#2b2b2e`, app `#ffffff`, red-orange `#ff3d00` (text `#d42f00`), grey `#6b6b6b`, soft band `#f6f6f6`.
- Motion density: low; scroll-snap carousel and tab swaps.

## Techniques

- Shared product-art kit for food imagery.
- Mobile-shell layout with a fixed bottom tab bar constrained to the column width.

## Layout Floor

One centered column with a sticky header and a fixed bottom tab bar; content padding keeps the last items clear of the bar. The capture's promotional modal is omitted.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 3 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/catchtable.jpg`).

- Matches: a phone-width white column centered on a dark desk, a sticky header with red circle logo, pill search, and two icons, an underlined tab row, a large rounded hero card carousel that peeks the next card, a 5x2 quick-menu icon grid with small red badges, curated sections with titles, grey subtitles, and horizontally scrolling cards, and a fixed bottom tab bar.
- Deliberate differences: the promotional modal that covers every capture frame is omitted; a small "Unofficial" note sits on the desk at wide widths; restaurants and dishes are invented and drawn.
- Remaining gaps: the study has three curated sections (2,028px) against the original's long feed (6,013px).
