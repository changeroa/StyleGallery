How these were made

Captured, then rewritten. Each page starts from a scroll capture of the original site and a scene-by-scene description. The study keeps the structure and motion but writes every word again in the brand's register.

Drawn, not copied. Photos, renders, and logos are replaced by CSS and SVG drawings, painted gradients, and invented names.

Checked on every page. Each study passes the showcase checks for overflow, focus, contrast, reduced motion, offline and no-script reading, and honest buttons, and its brief records a side-by-side comparison with the source frames.

Unofficial. These are design studies. They are not affiliated with or endorsed by any of the brands they study.

Unofficial brand studies by StyleGallery. Brand names are used only to identify the page being studied.
