# flex, unofficial study

## Direction

Reference: the captured flex.team AX landing. Mood: black stage, one explosion of warm color, and a single fluorescent green used only on the key word of each headline. Signature: the hero prompt types its own questions, and the data diagram actually flows into the engine.

## System

- Type: Pretendard Variable, 800 centered headlines with one green emphasized phrase each.
- Palette: ground `#000000`, raised `#121214`, text `#f4f4f5`, secondary `#b8b8be`, neon `#3ee07a`; hero bands coral `#ff5a3c`, orange `#ff8a1f`, yellow `#ffd166`, lilac `#c9b6ff`.
- Texture: SVG fractal-noise grain at 0.55 overlay on the hero.
- Motion density: hero band sway, prompt typing loop, dot wave, meter growth, alert slide-in, dashed flow lines.

## Techniques

- [Grain and texture](../../expression/techniques/grain-and-texture.md) and [Gradient atmosphere](../../expression/techniques/gradient-atmosphere.md) for the hero.
- Canvas dot wave gated by visibility.

## Layout Floor

Sticky notice plus header; the fixed round button reserves bottom scroll padding. Dual panels and cards stack below 760px.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 7 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/flex-team.jpg`).

- Matches: black NOTICE strip and dark header with outlined login and white pill CTA; a grainy hero of melted vertical color bands with a small eyebrow, big white headline, two-line sub, and a translucent prompt pill with a return glyph; a grey logo row; a large quoted question over a dot wave; green-keyword section headlines over two overlapping dark chat panels with progress meters; a dark app window with a sidebar; a terracotta-to-steel diagram panel with a row of nodes; three dark cards; a two-button closing with a neon green primary.
- Deliberate differences: all copy newly written; customer names invented; the prompt types itself; diagram lines flow.
- Remaining gaps: the original hero's bands are softer and more pastel near the edges; study 4,196px vs 9,139px because the footer sitemap is replaced by one line.
