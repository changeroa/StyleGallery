# Warp, unofficial study

## Direction

Reference: the captured warp.dev home (2026-09-21, 1440x900, 7,628px). Mood: engineering drawing - dot grid, hairlines, lowercase mono, one royal blue. Signature: the hero figure is a living factory floor where slots flip between running, waiting, and shipped while a counter climbs.

## System

- Type: IBM Plex Mono throughout; Inter only inside layer bars.
- Palette: paper `#fbfbfd` with `#d6d7e4` dots, ink `#14163a`, grey `#55586e`, blue `#2f31e6` (text `#2224b8`), lime `#e7f56a`, figure lavender `#f1f1f8`.
- Motion density: factory tick (visible only), LIVE pulse, chart draw, queue rotation, header theme flip.

## Techniques

- `[ fig. N · title ]` figure headers with rule lines, as in the capture.
- Header theme switch once the hero scrolls away.

## Layout Floor

Sticky header; the factory grid halves its columns below 700px; figure panels and product cards stack below 760px.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 8 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/warp.jpg`).

- Matches: dot-grid paper with column hairlines, lowercase mono throughout, a three-line hero with blue sub, dark and outlined buttons, a lime demo button; a `[ fig. N · title ]` figure panel with a LIVE pill over a grid of slots; the header switching to a royal-blue anchor bar with bracketed letter keys after the hero; a numbered pipeline list beside an output panel; three quality charts with PASS/BEST/AUTO-FIX badges; a blue case band; stepped layer bars ending in a solid blue layer; a cost chart plus queue; three product columns with dark shots; an FAQ with [+] markers.
- Deliberate differences: the factory grid animates (the capture's slots are sparse and static-looking); customers, numbers, and case studies are fictional.
- Remaining gaps: the original's factory grid is sparser and quieter; the study reads busier at first glance.
