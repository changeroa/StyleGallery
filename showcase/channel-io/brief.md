# Channel Talk (채널톡), unofficial study

## Direction

Reference: the captured channel.io/ko home. Mood: soft, optimistic, pastel light with black pill buttons. Signature: the AI input is live - pick a chip or type a situation and a demo answer types itself into a status region.

## System

- Type: Pretendard Variable, 800 headlines at -0.035em; eyebrow labels in grey.
- Palette: ground `#ffffff`, warm cards `#f7f5f1`, violet `#6a5cff`, ink `#1c1c20`; pastel stages pink/lilac/sky radial blends.
- Motion density: split-word hero, count-up stats, typed AI answer, card reveals.

## Techniques

- [Gradient atmosphere](../../expression/techniques/gradient-atmosphere.md) for pastel stages.
- Gradient-border input via padding-box/border-box backgrounds.

## Layout Floor

Sticky header under a promo strip; a fixed chat launcher with a dismissible bubble and bottom scroll padding. Splits and grids stack below 860px.
