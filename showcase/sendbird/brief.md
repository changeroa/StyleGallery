# Sendbird, unofficial study

## Direction

Reference: the captured sendbird.com home, a short page with one headline and two product cards. Signature: both cards are alive - the concierge memory keeps recalling new facts, and the API card's call timer runs.

## System

- Type: Inter Tight 500 at -0.045em for display, JetBrains Mono for code.
- Palette: ground `#ffffff`, ink `#0d0d0f`, grey card `#eef0f3`, concierge gradient `#a9c3e6 -> #e9eef8` with coral `#ff9a7a` bloom.
- Motion density: split-word headline, memory rows popping in (visible only), timer, stat counters.

## Techniques

- [Gradient atmosphere](../../expression/techniques/gradient-atmosphere.md) for the coral bloom.

## Layout Floor

Sticky header that gains a hairline once scrolled; the card pair stacks below 900px. The capture's cookie banner is omitted; a stats row extends the short original.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 2 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/sendbird.jpg`).

- Matches: a small bordered logo button, EN, Log in, and a black Contact sales button; a large centered two-line headline; two equal rounded cards - left in a blue gradient with a white Explore button and a frosted memory panel over a coral bloom, right in pale grey with a phone and a dark code block overlapping its corner.
- Deliberate differences: product names are generic (AI Concierge, Communications API); the phone shows abstract light instead of a support agent photo; the cookie banner over the capture is omitted; a stats row extends the page.
- Remaining gaps: the original's photography of people makes the API card warmer.
