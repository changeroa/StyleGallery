# KakaoBank, unofficial study

## Direction

Reference: the captured kakaobank.com home. Mood: calm, grey-on-white cards after one warm, dark cinematic hero. Signature: the hero's phone is not footage; its AI chat types a real answer, and the phone drifts up as you leave.

## System

- Type: Pretendard Variable, 700 headlines at -0.035em, 16px body, `keep-all` breaks.
- Palette: ground `#ffffff`, card `#f4f5f7`, ink `#1a1a1a`, secondary `#555b63`, yellow `#fee500` (chat and calendar only), chart teal `#2a9fb5`, hero browns `#7a4a3a -> #1c1312`.
- Texture: none; painted gradient panels stand in for photographs.
- Motion density: hero typing plus drift, collage parallax at three rates, chart line draw, calendar flip on carousel change.

## Techniques

- [Gradient atmosphere](../../expression/techniques/gradient-atmosphere.md) for painted panels.
- [Scroll choreography](../../motion/techniques/scroll-choreography.md) for scroll-derived parallax.

## Layout Floor

The document scrolls; the header sits on the hero and scrolls away. The collage becomes one column below 900px; the carousel stacks its dark stage under the copy.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 4 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/kakaobank.jpg`).

- Matches: full-bleed dark hero with a phone held close to camera and a white two-line centered headline, chevron at the bottom; three staggered service cards mixing tilted photos with grey UI panels; a three-tile facts row (chart, room, AAA on green); a two-part carousel with grey copy panel, arrows, black button, and a dark stage with a spotlit day calendar; a 2x3 grey link grid; a grey sitemap footer.
- Deliberate differences: copy is newly written; photography and video are painted gradients; the hero phone renders a live typing answer with slight depth-of-field blur instead of footage. Study height 3,802px vs 4,257px.
- Remaining gaps: the original's photographic people and money-stack render are not reproduced, so the hero reads more graphic than cinematic.
