# Cursor, unofficial study

## Direction

Reference: the captured cursor.com home (Korean locale, 2026-09-21, 1440x900, 8,151px). Mood: paper, clay, and a gallery wall of soft landscape paintings behind software. Signature: every product mockup sits on an original painted landscape made of layered gradients, SVG hills, and fractal grain.

## System

- Type: Pretendard Variable 500 at -0.03em; JetBrains Mono for terminals and file chips.
- Palette: ground `#f7f6f2`, card `#ecebe5`, ink `#26251e`, grey `#5d5b52`, link orange `#b54708`; landscape skies in sage, steel, sunset, and teal.
- Motion density: plan checklist ticking, terminal caret, model picker, split closing headline.

## Techniques

- [Grain and texture](../../expression/techniques/grain-and-texture.md) with multiply-blended SVG turbulence on painted panels.
- Two-tone feature headlines (ink sentence, grey continuation).

## Layout Floor

Sticky blurred header; the app mockup collapses to one column and the terminal joins the flow below 900px; feature rows and card trios stack.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 5 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/cursor.jpg`).

- Matches: warm off-white ground with a thin sticky header and dark pill download; a small two-line left-aligned promise with dark and grey pill CTAs; a wide painted landscape panel holding a three-pane desktop app (tasks, agent chat, browser preview) and an overlapping dark CLI window; feature rows on warm grey cards with two-tone text left and a painted panel with a floating mockup right; three-column cards (model picker, mobile, enterprise); a changelog strip; a centered quote row with a hairline logo grid.
- Deliberate differences: landscapes are original gradient-and-SVG paintings with grain rather than artworks; the model picker and plan checklist are interactive; the research-team photo section is omitted.
- Remaining gaps: the original's painted panels have visible brushwork; the study's are smoother.
