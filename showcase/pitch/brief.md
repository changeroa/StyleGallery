# Pitch, unofficial study

## Direction

Reference: the captured pitch.com home (2026-09-21, 1440x900, 14,350px). Mood: electric violet night into lavender daylight, with lime as the one loud note. Signature: the hero prompt actually drafts slides - type a topic and four thumbnails appear under it.

## System

- Type: Plus Jakarta Sans 800 at -0.05em for the hero, IBM Plex Mono uppercase section labels.
- Palette: night `#1d0f4f`, violet `#6b3cf0`, lavender `#c6b3ff`, soft lavender `#f3eeff`, lime `#c9f26a`, ink `#150b35`.
- Motion density: split hero, drifting slides (paused offscreen), deck draft, stage swaps, tab swaps.

## Techniques

- White, lime, and navy pill buttons with trailing arrows.
- Sticky visual stage beside a feature list; sideways snap rail for steps.

## Layout Floor

Sticky note plus nav with 110px scroll padding; the workspace stage stops sticking below 900px; the wall goes 4, 2, 1 columns; dark sections stack.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 8 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/pitch.jpg`).

- Matches: a lavender note strip over a dark violet nav with white and lime pills; a violet hero with blurred template slides drifting behind a giant two-line white headline and a white prompt box; a customer name row; a how-to section on lavender with numbered large cards; a colored quote wall (violet, lime, dark) with a centered "loved by" cell; a toolkit bento on soft lavender with one dark analytics card; a dark section with a centered lavender two-line head, underlined solution tabs, an image-and-copy panel; a "Templates (150+)" block beside large uppercase category words; an FAQ of rounded rows; a giant closing word with a white footer card holding a dark CTA box.
- Deliberate differences: the prompt drafts four slide thumbnails from your text; the workspace feature list swaps a sticky slide; tabs and categories are live; quotes and customers are fictional; portraits become colored blocks.
- Remaining gaps: study 5,779px vs 14,350px; the original's sticky workspace section with three feature visuals is condensed to one stage.
