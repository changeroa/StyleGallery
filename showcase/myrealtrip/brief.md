# Myrealtrip, unofficial study

## Direction

Reference: the captured myrealtrip.com home. Mood: practical travel shopping on white, generous card imagery, blue sign-in. Signature: the city name heading is a live control - changing it redraws the ranking, the section titles, and the product scenes in that city's palette.

## System

- Type: Pretendard Variable, 800 city heading at 26px, 700 prices with red discounts.
- Palette: ground `#ffffff`, blue `#1c7ed6`, ink `#141719`, soft `#f1f3f5`, sale `#e03131`, badge lilac `#f3e8ff`.
- Motion density: card image zoom, city redraw, star toggles.

## Techniques

- Painted city scenes composed from the shared art kit; each city has its own three-color palette.

## Layout Floor

Static header; banners go to one column and grids to two below 860px; the ranking drops to one column below 760px. The capture's rotating ad modal is omitted.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 6 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/myrealtrip.jpg`).

- Matches: header with the name, an overseas/domestic pill toggle, a search field, and a blue sign-in button; a category row; three rounded ad banners; a large city-name heading with a caret over a chip row and a two-column numbered ranking with square thumbnails and red discounts; a row of round quick links; four-column product grids with badges, save marks, prices, and star ratings.
- Deliberate differences: the city heading is a working select that redraws the ranking and grids; the rotating ad modal over every capture frame is omitted; all products are invented and drawn.
- Remaining gaps: study 2,327px vs 6,862px; the original has more product sections and a travel-info block before the footer.
