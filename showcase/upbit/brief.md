# Upbit, unofficial study

## Direction

Reference: the captured upbit.com home. Mood: institutional, navy and white, data first. Signature: the market is alive - a fictional treemap and ranking table tick every second using the Korean convention (red up, blue down) and flash on each change.

## System

- Type: Pretendard Variable with tabular numerals.
- Palette: ground `#ffffff`, navy `#093687`, blue `#115dcb`, up `#c84a31`, down `#1261c4`, strip `#eef1f6`.
- Motion density: 1s data tick while visible, row flash, chart line draw in the service tab.

## Techniques

- Accessible tabs from the shared kit for ranking and services.
- All market values are fictional and labeled as such.

## Layout Floor

The document scrolls under a fixed 58px header; a small fixed QR box sits bottom-right on wide screens. The capture's full-screen promo popup is intentionally omitted.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 4 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/upbit.jpg`).

- Matches: the three-line identity with a blue last line and navy plus blue square buttons, a red and blue treemap filling the right two thirds, the grey index strip, the ranking table beside notices and a blue warning block, underlined service tabs over a soft curve with a phone mockup and a day calendar, three bordered security cards, and a bottom-right QR box.
- Deliberate differences: every number is fictional and animated; asset names are invented Korean words; the full-screen promotional popup that dims every capture frame is omitted.
- Remaining gaps: the original's footer carries dense legal copy and partner seals that the study reduces to one line.
