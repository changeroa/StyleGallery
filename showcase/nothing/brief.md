## Direction

Nothing's register: monochrome paper and ink, dot-matrix display type, mono uppercase labels, see-through hardware, and a single red accent used like an indicator light. Backgrounds shift tint per section the way the capture's blurred page does (pale yellow, grey, concentric grain, faint green and lilac).

## System

- Type: Doto (dot display), Space Mono (labels), Inter (body).
- Color: paper #f2f2f0, ink #111, red #d71921 for indicators only.
- Hardware is CSS shapes; no product photography or logos. The wordmark is plain dot type, not the brand logo.

## Techniques

- Playable glyph lights: a pressed-button group drives idle, call, charging, and recording patterns on a drawn phone back.
- Dot-matrix board: an input redraws a 5x7 dot font across the board with a column-staggered sweep.
- Live dot clock on the watch card; count-up specs; section tint changes as the scroll signal.
- The consent dialog that covered the capture becomes a non-blocking card with honest demo actions.

## Layout Floor

Semantic sections with a sticky header; the board input has a label and a polite live status; patterns stop animating under reduced motion; no content is behind an overlay.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 8 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/nothing.jpg`). Every capture frame is covered by a consent dialog over a blurred page, so only the dialog, the dot-matrix wordmark, and the per-section background tint are observable.

- Matches: a dot-matrix uppercase wordmark; the dialog's shape (wordmark, paragraph, bold subhead, three equal buttons with the last one black), kept as a non-blocking card near the end; section grounds that follow the capture's tint sequence (pale yellow at the top, grey, near-white with concentric grain, faint green and lilac, grey at the bottom).
- Deliberate differences: the page body, which the capture never shows, is built from the brand's public design language: a transparent phone with playable glyph lights, a dot board that redraws typed words, drawn earbuds, watch, and speaker cards, and invented specs. No logo, product photography, or copy is reused.
- Remaining gaps: because the real body content was not observable, structure below the hero cannot be verified against the original; study 3,509px vs 7,266px.
