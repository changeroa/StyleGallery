# Banksalad, unofficial study

## Direction

Reference: the captured banksalad.com home. Mood: friendly and clean, mint and white, with flat illustration. Signature: the hero is alive - rings ripple outward and five money objects orbit an original leaf character while the four-dot pager rotates headlines.

## System

- Type: Pretendard Variable, 800 hero at -0.045em, 15px body.
- Palette: ground `#ffffff`, mint `#e9f8f2`, green `#00a676`, ink `#1e2124`, grey `#5b6168`, app band browns `#6b4a33 -> #2c1e16`.
- Motion density: ripple loop, orbit loop, auto pager (visible only), phone parallax, partner marquee.

## Techniques

- [Gradient atmosphere](../../expression/techniques/gradient-atmosphere.md) for the app band.
- Shared kit marquee and ambient pausing.

## Layout Floor

The document scrolls under a fixed 64px white header. Hero collapses to one column at 760px; the pick row goes to two columns; the phone hides.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 2 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/banksalad.jpg`).

- Matches: white fixed header with a small nav, mint hero with concentric white rings, a two-line bold headline with a filled and an outlined green pill stacked, flat money objects around a round portrait, a four-dot pager with a long active dot, and a centered two-column comparison promise below.
- Deliberate differences: the portrait is an original leaf character; headlines and body are new copy; partner marks are fictional names in a marquee. Rings ripple and objects orbit; the capture shows them static.
- Remaining gaps: the original hero is slightly taller with larger illustration; the study's dark app band uses a painted gradient instead of a photographed hand.
