# Yanolja (NOL), unofficial study

## Direction

Reference: the captured nol.yanolja.com home. Mood: busy but orderly leisure marketplace, white with blue accents and big rounded photo cards. Signature: the genre pills really switch the ranking, and every poster is a newly designed typographic poster for an invented show.

## System

- Type: Pretendard Variable, 800 section titles at 20px with an inline grey subtitle; the name NOL is plain text.
- Palette: ground `#ffffff`, blue `#1f5bff`, ink `#1a1a1a`, soft `#f5f6f8`, sale `#e03b2c`, deal band `#e9eef8 -> #f7f9fc`.
- Motion density: hero slide, poster lift, ranking swap, back-to-top reveal.

## Techniques

- Painted travel scenes and original posters built from SVG and the shared art kit.

## Layout Floor

Sticky header; the hero track clips inside its section. Ranking goes from five to three columns and the card grids to one or two below 900px. The capture's festival popup is omitted.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 4 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/yanolja.jpg`).

- Matches: sticky header with the NOL name and a pill search; a wide rounded hero card that peeks the next slide; a notice strip and a sign-up band; a row of colorful category icons; a genre ranking with black-active pills and five numbered posters; photo card grids; a pale blue special-deal band; a three-column promo card grid with dark scrims.
- Deliberate differences: every poster is an original typographic poster for an invented show (no real productions or artwork); photography is painted scenes; the festival popup over every capture frame is omitted.
- Remaining gaps: the original packs more rows (6,652px vs 2,902px) and uses photographic hotel imagery.
