# Figma, unofficial study

## Direction

Reference: the captured figma.com home (Korean locale). Mood: white page, bold single-color stage panels, black buttons. Signature: every stage performs its feature - cursors move, the prompt types and the app assembles, and pressing the repo button ticks the checklist.

## System

- Type: Pretendard Variable 500 headings; two-tone section heads (black sentence, grey continuation).
- Palette: white, black, grey `#6b6b6b`, lime stage `#d6f37a`, grey stage `#e8e8e8`, cyan stage `#b8efff`, generated-app blue `#2a3bd8`.
- Motion density: dot-matrix spell-in, cursor drift, prompt typing with row reveals, pen path draw, checklist ticks.

## Techniques

- SVG tool tiles including a self-drawing pen path.
- The brand name appears as plain text; no logo mark or brand color set is used.

## Layout Floor

Sticky header; hero stacks below 960px; tiles go four to two columns; the cookie banner over the capture is omitted.

## Comparison With The Original

Reviewed side by side at 1440x900 against 8 capture frames (`.tmp/compare/figma.jpg`).

- Matches: a white sticky header with small links, a text login, an outlined sales button, and a black primary button; a three-line left hero beside a tilted collage around a violet dot-matrix poster, with a large black start button on the right; two-tone section heads (black sentence, grey continuation) indented over full-width single-color stage panels (lime with devices and named cursors, grey with a prompt bar and a generated blue app screen, cyan with a repo input); a tight grid of tool demo tiles; a quote beside a big percentage and a wordmark row; a resource card row; a black footer.
- Deliberate differences: the poster spells an invented word in lit dots; cursors drift, the prompt types and the app rows assemble, and the repo button ticks a checklist; the brand mark and logo colors are not used; quotes, customers, and numbers are fictional; the cookie banner over every capture frame is omitted.
- Remaining gaps: study 5,294px vs 9,279px; the original's stage mockups (lime and grey) are denser and photographic, while the study's lime stage reads sparse; the template carousel uses drawn icons instead of real templates.
